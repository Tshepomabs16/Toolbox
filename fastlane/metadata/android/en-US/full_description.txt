Toolbox is a document toolkit that does all its work on your phone. Your files are never uploaded anywhere, because the app has no internet permission at all.

Most free scanner and PDF apps send your documents (IDs, payslips, bank statements, contracts) to a server to process them. Toolbox can't: there is no account, no cloud, no ads and no analytics.

<b>What it does</b>

• <b>Scan to PDF</b>: photograph pages with automatic edge detection and cropping, or import photos. Choose Original, Grayscale or a crisp black-and-white filter, rotate or remove pages, and export a multi-page PDF.
• <b>Split</b>: pull pages out of a PDF by range, like 1-3, 7, 10-12.
• <b>Merge</b>: combine several PDFs into one, in the order you choose.
• <b>Compress</b>: shrink a PDF or image to 1, 2 or 5 MB, or your own limit. Ideal for upload portals with a size cap. Toolbox never hands back a file bigger than the one you started with.
• <b>OCR</b>: copy the text out of a photo or screenshot. The text recognition runs on your phone.
• <b>Files</b>: everything you make is kept in one place to search, open, share, save or delete.

<b>Start from any app</b>
Share a PDF or image to Toolbox, or pick it under "Open with", and it offers the tools that fit.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No internet permission, so the app physically cannot send your files anywhere
• No camera permission: scanning uses Google Play services' document scanner, which hands back only the pages you capture
• No account, no ads, no tracking, no watermark
• Open source: anyone can check the code

Toolbox is free and open source under the MIT license.
